What availability, remote, and pay mean

Results are records from the named snapshot, not live availability checks. The default includes recently checked and stale records; Availability evidence lets you choose exact snapshot states.

Remote match uses source flags plus broad title and location clues. Workplace type uses the board's classification. The pay filter matches when a published range reaches the annual amount; currencies are not converted.

Filters are kept in this page's URL for reload, Back, and sharing. Shared URLs can reveal search terms; saved searches and visit history stay on this device.
